Restore Purchases

Eligible non-consumable purchases and active subscriptions can be restored when you are signed in with the same store account that originally made the purchase.

iPhone / iPad

Open HexaBliss, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Restoration does not charge you again.

Android

Eligible owned purchases are normally synchronized automatically through Google Play Billing when the game connects to Google Play. Make sure you are signed in to the same Google account used for the original purchase.

Important

Consumable items that have already been consumed are generally not restorable. If an eligible purchase is still missing, email us with your device platform and a brief description of the issue. Please do not send full payment-card information.
